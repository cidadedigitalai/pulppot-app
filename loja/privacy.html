Privacy policy

Pulp Pot, the Brazilian fruit merging game

In one sentence: Pulp Pot does not collect, send or share any of your data.

Last updated October 24, 2026

What data the app collects

None. Pulp Pot:

  • does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal information;
  • has no sign-up, login, account or password;
  • shows no ads and has no in-app purchases;
  • does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use the device advertising identifier;
  • does not use analytics, statistics or crash-reporting tools;
  • does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or files.

Device permissions

The app asks for no permissions. The vibration when fruits merge, when you reach a goal and when a game ends uses the standard iPhone feature and needs no authorization.

What is stored on your device

  • Your best score.
  • The trees planted in your orchard and the fruits already discovered in the album.
  • The game in progress (where each fruit is, the score and the goal), so you can continue where you left off.
  • Your language, sound effects and music settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information stays only on your device, is used only so the game can continue where you left off, and is never sent anywhere. To erase everything, just delete the app.

Internet and third-party services

The game works offline and makes no network connections. There are no third-party services built into the app. The links to this page, to support, to the terms and to Ararakú on the App Store open in your browser, outside the game.

Sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Children and teenagers

Pulp Pot is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children and teenagers, and is designed to be safe by default: no ads, no purchases, no prize boxes, no chatting with other people, no public profile and no user-generated content.

When you contact support

If you write to support on WhatsApp, your phone number and the messages you send stay with the developer only to answer you. They are not used for marketing or shared with anyone, and you can ask for the conversation to be deleted. WhatsApp is a Meta service and follows Meta's own privacy policy.

Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

Data protection laws such as Brazil's LGPD and the European Union's GDPR give you the right to know, correct and request deletion of your personal data. Since the developer keeps no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side: the game data stays with you, on your device. For any privacy question, contact support.

Browser version

The game's website also uses no cookies, ads or analytics tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the date updated at the top.
