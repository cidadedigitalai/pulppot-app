How do I play?

  • Drag your finger across the pot to aim and let go to drop the fruit.
  • Two matching fruits that touch merge into the next, bigger fruit.
  • The order is: açaí, jaboticaba, acerola, cashew apple, guava, passion fruit, mango, pineapple, jackfruit and watermelon. Two watermelons disappear with a bonus.
  • If the fruits pile up past the dashed line and stay there, the pot is full and the game ends. The line blinks red as a warning.

An animated tutorial plays the first time you open the game. To watch it again, open the settings (the sliders button at the top) and tap "Watch the tutorial".

What are the goals and the orchard?

Each game has a goal shown at the top, such as making a certain fruit or reaching a number of points. Reach it and a tree is planted in your orchard right away. The orchard has 10 trees, and the macaws from Ararakú come to visit as it grows. After the tenth tree, new goals keep coming. Losing a game never takes a tree away.

Where is the fruit album?

Tap the tree button at the top. Below the orchard, the album shows the fruits you have made; tap one to read a fun fact about it. Fruits you have not made yet appear as gray silhouettes.

Is there a time limit?

No. There is no clock: play at your own pace.

I closed the game in the middle of a match. Did I lose it?

No. The game in progress is saved automatically and continues where you left off.

How do I change the language?

Use the EN and PT buttons at the top of the game or in the settings. The game remembers your choice. On first launch it follows your iPhone's language.

How do I turn off the sound or the music?

Open the settings (the sliders button at the top) and turn off "Sound effects" or "Background music". The game remembers your choice.

How do I erase my progress and start over?

Your progress is saved only on your device. To start from scratch, delete the app and install it again.

I got a new iPhone. Does my progress come with me?

No. Since the game has no account and sends nothing over the internet, your progress stays on the device where you played.

Does it need internet? Are there purchases or ads?

It doesn't need internet, has no purchases and no ads.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later. On iPad, the game opens in iPhone mode.

What about my privacy?

Pulp Pot collects no data at all. See the privacy policy.
